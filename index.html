About the workshop

Agentic AI is reshaping software engineering — and must itself be engineered

Agentic AI marks a paradigm shift in the engineering of software-intensive systems. Unlike conventional, deterministic applications, agentic systems are decentralized software ecosystems in which specialized autonomous agents use Foundation Models — LLMs and SLMs — as cognitive reasoning engines, planning, deciding and learning to pursue shared goals through coordinated autonomy. This workshop explores the symbiotic relationship between Software Engineering and Agentic AI, in both directions.

Direction 01

Engineering with Agentic AI

Autonomous agents are transforming the software engineering lifecycle — accelerating development and automating complex operations, while making software-intensive systems significantly harder to validate, certify, maintain and evolve.

  • Agents across development and operations
  • Validating and certifying agent-built systems
  • Maintenance and evolution in the agentic era

Direction 02

Engineering of Agentic AI

The operational uncertainty inherent to agentic ecosystems demands new design abstractions, reference architectures and runtime engineering techniques to build, operate, govern and evolve resilient multi-agent systems.

  • Dynamic context management
  • Adaptive control and runtime V&V
  • Reference architectures for multi-agent systems

Why agentic systems are hard to engineer

  • Probabilistic

    Agents rely on probabilistic reasoning models, so their behaviour is non-deterministic by design.

  • Uncertain

    They depend on highly uncertain context: a volatile environment and the unpredictable, emergent behaviour of coordinating agents.

  • Stateful

    Yet they must reliably remember past actions, track multi-step progress toward shared goals, and keep context across agents, tools and users.

Goals

What the workshop sets out to do

The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners around the engineering of software-intensive systems in the Agentic AI era.

01

Map challenges and opportunities

Discuss the challenges and opportunities of engineering software-intensive systems with Agentic AI, across the full lifecycle.

02

Confront runtime uncertainty

Examine critical runtime challenges: dynamic context management — including memory staleness and context relevance — adaptive control, self-adaptation, runtime governance and viability-zone enforcement under high operational uncertainty.

03

Build a connected community

Connect researchers, industrial practitioners and students to foster cross-sector partnerships, joint research initiatives and collaborative networks at the intersection of Agentic AI and Software Engineering.
